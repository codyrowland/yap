Step 3 of 5

Download a model

Pick a model to run on this computer. Parakeet is the fastest on most PCs; Whisper covers more languages. More sizes are in Settings → On-device.

Connect a cloud provider

Audio is sent to the provider for each dictation and billed to your account. The key is kept in Windows Credential Manager, not in settings.

Connect a transcription server

Enter an OpenAI-compatible transcription endpoint. API keys are kept in Windows Credential Manager, not in settings.

Step 4 of 5

Set your talk keys

Press keys one at a time, then choose Save keys. Hold them together when dictating in other apps. Choose two different combinations.

Press Esc to cancel a recording without inserting text.

Step 5 of 5

Try it

With the cursor in the box below, hold , say a sentence, then let go. Yap types your words into the box — exactly the way it will in any other app. For longer passages, press once to start and again to stop.
